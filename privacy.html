Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Where your data is stored

Qarzaad has no server of its own. Your profile, ledger, debts and credit report summaries are stored on your iPhone and in your own iCloud account. Group data is stored in the group creator's iCloud and shared with members through Apple's iCloud sharing. You choose what each group can see.

Documents you upload

Before a document leaves your iPhone, the app blacks out Emirates ID, IBAN, account and card numbers, passport numbers, email addresses and street addresses. The redacted pages are then sent to Anthropic's Claude API to read the transactions and balances. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models. The original file is deleted after reading; only the extracted numbers are kept. Payment proof images are kept, redacted, as evidence in the group.

Google data

If the group creator connects Google, Qarzaad requests only the drive.file permission. With it the app can create and edit only the spreadsheet it creates, and cannot see any other file in your Drive. The app writes meeting notes and action items to that sheet and reads it back to include it in your analysis, which means its contents are sent to Anthropic's Claude API in the same way as your documents. The sign-in token is stored on your iPhone only.

Qarzaad's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except as described above.

What we do not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no selling of data, no accounts on our side.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and remove Qarzaad's data in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage. To revoke Google access, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.
